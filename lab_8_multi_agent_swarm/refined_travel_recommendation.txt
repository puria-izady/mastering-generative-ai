Travel to London is highly recommended due to its sunny weather, perfect for outdoor activities and sightseeing. Take a leisurely stroll through Hyde Park, enjoy a picnic, or visit the iconic Buckingham Palace. The pleasant weather also makes it an excellent time to explore London's many museums and galleries.

New York, on the other hand, is experiencing snowy conditions with temperatures at 3°C, making it less ideal for travel unless you are prepared for winter activities. If you are a fan of winter sports, consider visiting Central Park for ice skating or enjoy a cozy day exploring the city's renowned museums and Broadway shows.
